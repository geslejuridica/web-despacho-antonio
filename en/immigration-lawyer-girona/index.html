Nationality and immigration

Immigration and nationality in Girona and the Costa Brava

Living and working in Spain with your papers in order depends on deadlines, requirements and documents that vary in each case. I analyse your situation and explain which permit you can apply for, what you need to gather and when it is best to apply.

What I look at with you

  • Residence permits based on settlement (arraigo): social, socio-occupational, socio-educational, family or second-chance.
  • Renewal of residence and work permits, and long-term residence.
  • Family reunification and residence cards for family members of EU citizens.
  • Applications for Spanish nationality by residence.
  • Temporary protection for people displaced from Ukraine.
  • Appeals against refusals, closures of files and orders to leave Spain.

What you should know

  • Since 20 May 2025 a new Immigration Regulation (Royal Decree 1155/2024) has applied. It changed the types of settlement (arraigo) and reduced the required prior stay to two years.
  • Nationality by residence generally requires ten years of legal residence. This is reduced to two years for nationals of Ibero-American countries, Andorra, the Philippines, Equatorial Guinea and Portugal, and to one year in some cases, such as marriage to a Spanish national (Art. 22 of the Civil Code).
  • Nationality usually requires the Instituto Cervantes exams: DELE A2 and the constitutional and sociocultural knowledge test (CCSE).
  • A refusal can be appealed, but the deadlines are short: normally one month for an appeal for reconsideration and two months to go to court.

How I work

I review your documents and residence history, tell you which route has the best chances and what is missing, and we prepare the application or appeal together.
