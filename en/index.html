The approach

Every case deserves a full analysis, not a label.

People who come to me do not always know which procedure they need or which area of law affects their situation. That is why my first step is not to choose a specialism: it is to listen, analyse and assess.

A problem rarely belongs to just one area of law. A divorce can have civil, tax and property consequences; a dismissal can involve employment and social security issues; an inheritance can involve civil, tax and property matters. That is why I study your situation as a whole, identify all the areas involved and explain how they relate to one another.

I analyse your case to determine whether there is a viable legal route and explain the options, chances and risks before any decision is made.

Do you have a legal problem?

Start by telling it in your own words.

These are some of the ways people describe their situation when they come to the practice. None of them mentions an area of law, and yours does not need to either.

«I've been dismissed and I don't know if what they're offering me is right.»

«I've received a letter and I don't understand what they're claiming from me.»

«I have a disagreement with a family member and I don't know how to resolve it.»

«I signed something and now I think I shouldn't have.»

«They're claiming money from me that I don't think I owe.»

«I don't know if what has happened to me has a legal solution.»
